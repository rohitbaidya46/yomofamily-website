How it works

From group chat to one tap.

  1. Connect your wallet

    Link the wallet you already use. Your funds stay in your wallet.

  2. Follow your people

    Add friends and traders you trust. Build your trading family.

  3. See moves live

    Every buy and sell from people you follow shows up in your feed as it happens.

  4. Copy in one tap

    Like a trade? Tap copy, choose your amount, confirm. You approve every trade.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Yomo Family?

Yomo Family is a social trading app for crypto. You follow friends and traders, see their trades live, and copy the ones you like in one tap.

Does Yomo hold my crypto?

No. You connect your own wallet and approve every trade yourself.

Which chains will it support?

We are starting with Solana, Ethereum, Base and BNB Chain. Tell us on X which chain you want next.

When does the beta open?

Soon. People on the waitlist get access first, so join with your email above.

What does YOMO mean?

You Only Move Once. Make the move together, with your family of traders.
